# Qwen3-ASR-1.7B: audio-encoder MLP through the nkilib NKI kernel

- **Change:** `VLLM_NEURON_ASR_AUDIO_MLP_NKI` routes `Qwen3ASRAudioMLP` (up → GELU → down) to `NF.mlp(skip_gate=True, act_fn=GELU)`.
- **Author:** Dax (Daxrajsinh-holboxai). Original PR: https://github.com/susmit-holbox/covex-neuron/pull/2
- **Baseline:** vllm-neuron 0.29.0.1.2.0, raw aten audio MLP.
- **Setup:**
  - trn2.48xlarge, Neuron SDK 2.33.1, BF16
  - decoder TP8 + audio encoder DP8
  - LibriSpeech, 32 samples, concurrency 1 to 8

| Metric | Baseline | NKI | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| E2E latency p50, concurrency 1 | 0.103 s | 0.091 s | −11.6% |
| RTF mean, concurrency 1 | 0.020 | 0.017 | −14.0% |
| TTFT p50, concurrency 1 | 0.051 s | 0.038 s | −25.0% |
| Throughput, concurrency 8 | 136 audio-s/s | 160.7 audio-s/s | +18.1% |

- E2E latency improves at every concurrency, from −11.6% at concurrency 1 to −26.6% at concurrency 8.
- TTFT improves from −25% at concurrency 1 to −46% at concurrency 8.
- ITL is unchanged, as expected: the text decoder is untouched.
- WER goes from 2.45% to 2.53%.

**Status:** ported into COVEX Neuron as `port/qwen3-asr-audio-mlp-nki`, default **on** for Qwen3-ASR. `VLLM_NEURON_ASR_AUDIO_MLP_NKI=0` restores stock behavior.
